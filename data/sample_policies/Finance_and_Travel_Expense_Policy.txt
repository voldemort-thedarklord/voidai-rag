ACME ENTERPRISE CORP - FINANCE & ACCOUNTS DEPARTMENT
DOCUMENT ID: FIN-EXP-2025-V4
TITLE: Corporate Travel, Entertainment, and Expense Reimbursement Policy
EFFECTIVE DATE: March 1, 2025
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Restricted (Finance & Executive Only)

1. OVERVIEW AND GENERAL PRINCIPLES
This policy governs reimbursement of reasonable business expenses incurred by employees on behalf of Acme Enterprise Corp. The company reimburses expenses that are necessary, directly business-related, and substantiated by valid itemized tax receipts.

2. AIR TRAVEL AND LODGING
2.1 Air Travel: All domestic flights under six (6) hours flight duration must be booked in Economy / Coach Class. Flights exceeding six continuous hours or international long-haul flights qualify for Premium Economy. Business Class booking is strictly reserved for C-suite Executives and Vice Presidents.
2.2 Flight Bookings: Bookings must be initiated at least fourteen (14) days prior to the travel date via the Acme Corporate Travel Portal (Navan). Domestic airfare exceeding $500 requires written pre-authorization from the Department Vice President.
2.3 Hotel Accommodation: The maximum allowable hotel room rate is $220/night in Tier-1 cities (San Francisco, New York, London, Tokyo) and $150/night in all other destinations, exclusive of statutory taxes.

3. MEALS AND PER DIEM ALLOWANCES
3.1 Daily Meal Allowance: Employees on approved overnight business trips may claim up to a maximum daily meal per-diem of $75 per day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner).
3.2 Alcohol Reimbursement: Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable for individual dining. Reasonable alcoholic beverage charges incurred during documented client entertainment dinners with prior Director-level approval are reimbursable.
3.3 Client Entertainment: Detailed attendee lists, client company names, and specific business purposes must be attached to the expense claim. Expenses exceeding $100 per attendee require prior VP approval.

4. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS AND SUBMISSION DEADLINES
4.1 All expense claims must be filed through the Concur expense system within thirty (30) calendar days of expenditure. Claims filed past 60 days will be rejected and treated as personal non-reimbursable expenses.
4.2 Itemized receipts are required for any individual transaction exceeding $25. Credit card charge slips showing only the final total without itemization do not qualify as adequate substantiation.
4.3 Reimbursements are processed on bi-weekly payroll cycles following managerial sign-off.
