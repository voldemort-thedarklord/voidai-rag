ACME ENTERPRISE CORP - BOARD OF DIRECTORS & EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE
DOCUMENT ID: EXEC-COMP-CONFIDENTIAL-2025
TITLE: Executive Leadership Compensation, Equity Vesting, and Bonus Multiplier Framework
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2025
CLASSIFICATION: STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL (Executive Committee Only)

1. EXECUTIVE BONUS MULTIPLIER MATRIX
1.1 The Annual Executive Incentive Bonus is computed using the following formula:
    Bonus Payout = Base Salary x Target Percentage x (0.5 x Company KPI Multiplier + 0.5 x Individual Scorecard)
1.2 C-Suite Executives (CEO, CTO, CFO, CRO): Base target bonus is 50% of annual base salary.
1.3 Vice Presidents (VP): Base target bonus is 35% of annual base salary.
1.4 Senior Directors: Base target bonus is 25% of annual base salary.
1.5 Maximum Cap: Under no circumstances may the consolidated bonus multiplier exceed 2.0x (200% of target bonus), regardless of overachievement in revenue or EBITDA margins.

2. EQUITY VESTING AND GOLDEN PARACHUTES
2.1 All executive stock options and Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) adhere to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff (25% vesting at month 12, followed by quarterly pro-rata vesting over months 13 to 48).
2.2 Change in Control (Double-Trigger Acceleration): In the event of an acquisition or corporate merger where the executive is involuntarily terminated without cause within 12 months post-acquisition, 100% of unvested equity options accelerate immediately.
2.3 Executive Severance: VPs and C-Suite members terminated without cause receive 9 months base salary continuation, full health benefits continuation (COBRA), and prorated annual bonus.

3. RETENTION LOCK-INS
3.1 Key executive retention bonuses paid upon signing are subject to a 24-month clawback clause if the officer voluntarily resigns before completion of 24 full months.
