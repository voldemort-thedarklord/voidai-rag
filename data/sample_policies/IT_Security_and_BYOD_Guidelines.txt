ACME ENTERPRISE CORP - INFORMATION SECURITY & INFRASTRUCTURE
DOCUMENT ID: SEC-STD-2025-01
TITLE: Information Security, Acceptable Use, and BYOD Standards
EFFECTIVE DATE: February 1, 2025
CLASSIFICATION: Internal Restricted

1. ACCESS CONTROL AND PASSWORDS
1.1 Passwords for all Acme enterprise accounts must be at least 14 characters in length and include uppercase, lowercase, numerical, and special characters.
1.2 Password expiration cycles are set to 90 days. Users may not reuse any of their last 6 previous passwords.
1.3 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via hardware security key (FIDO2) or authenticator app (e.g., Okta Verify, Google Authenticator) is mandatory on all corporate single sign-on (SSO) gateways. SMS-based 2FA is explicitly forbidden due to SIM-swapping vulnerabilities.

2. DEVICE MANAGEMENT AND BYOD (BRING YOUR OWN DEVICE)
2.1 All corporate-issued laptops must have Full Disk Encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) enabled with keys escrowed in Acme Mobile Device Management (MDM - Kandji/InTune).
2.2 Personal devices (smartphones/tablets) accessing corporate email, Slack, or GitHub must install Acme's endpoint management profile. Devices jailbroken or rooted are strictly banned and will be blocked automatically at the zero-trust perimeter.
2.3 In the event of a lost or stolen device, the employee must report the incident to the Security Operations Center (soc@acme-corp.internal) within two (2) hours of discovery. An immediate remote cryptographic wipe will be initiated.

3. SOFTWARE, SHADOW IT, AND AI TOOLS
3.1 Employees are prohibited from installing unvetted third-party software, browser extensions, or utilities without written authorization from the IT Architecture Review Board.
3.2 Proprietary corporate source code, customer personal data (PII), and financial datasets must NEVER be pasted into public, non-enterprise generative AI tools (such as public ChatGPT or free online converters). Only company-sanctioned internal enterprise RAG assistants and approved enterprise tenants may process internal data.
3.3 Cloud storage services (such as personal Dropbox, Google Drive, or iCloud) are blocked on all managed networks and endpoints.

4. REMOTE CONNECTIVITY AND VPN
4.1 Public Wi-Fi networks (cafes, airports, hotels) must only be accessed through the Acme WireGuard Zero-Trust Network Access (ZTNA) tunnel.
4.2 Split tunneling is disabled on all remote VPN profiles to enforce full security inspection.
